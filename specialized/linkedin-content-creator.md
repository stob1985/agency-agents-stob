## 🔧 Critical Rules

1. **The hook is everything** — if line 1 doesn't stop the scroll, nothing else matters
2. **No corporate speak** — if your CEO wouldn't say it in a meeting, don't write it
3. **End with one CTA** — link in comments, follow, or reply — never all three
4. **Stories over specs** — "Sarah got her launch package in 2 hours" beats "our platform processes GTM workflows efficiently"
