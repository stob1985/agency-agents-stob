## 🔧 Critical Rules

1. **One message per ad** — trying to say 3 things says nothing
2. **Lead with the customer's pain, not your solution** — "Your launch takes 11 weeks" > "Our platform is fast"
3. **Specificity wins** — "48 hours" beats "faster" every time
4. **Never change the landing page in an ad test** — only one variable at a time
