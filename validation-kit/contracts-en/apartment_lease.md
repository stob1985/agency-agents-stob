# RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

**Property Address:** 245 East 86th Street, Apt 4B, New York, NY 10028
**Lease Term:** 12 months
**Lease Start Date:** February 1, 2026
**Monthly Rent:** $3,800

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made between:

**LANDLORD:** Empire Property Holdings LLC, 500 Madison Avenue, New York, NY 10022
**TENANT:** [TENANT NAME]

---

## 1. RENT

1.1 Tenant agrees to pay $3,800 per month, due on the first of each month.

1.2 A late fee of $150 shall apply if rent is not received by the 3rd of the month. Additional late fees of $25 per day shall accrue thereafter.

1.3 Landlord may increase rent at any time upon thirty (30) days written notice, in Landlord's sole and absolute discretion.

1.4 All payments shall be made via certified check, money order, or wire transfer. Personal checks and electronic payments are not accepted. Cash payments may be accepted at Landlord's discretion with a $50 processing fee.

## 2. SECURITY DEPOSIT

2.1 Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $11,400 (equivalent to three months' rent) prior to taking possession of the Premises.

2.2 The security deposit shall be held by Landlord without interest in a non-segregated account.

2.3 Landlord may deduct from the security deposit for any damages, cleaning costs, repairs, repainting, professional carpet cleaning (required regardless of carpet condition), unpaid rent, late fees, attorney fees, or any other amounts Landlord deems necessary in its sole discretion.

2.4 If the security deposit is insufficient to cover deductions, Tenant remains liable for the balance.

2.5 The security deposit will be returned, less any deductions, within ninety (90) days after Tenant vacates the Premises, provided all conditions are met.

## 3. UTILITIES AND FEES

3.1 Tenant shall pay all utilities including electricity, gas, water, sewer, internet, and cable.

3.2 Tenant shall pay a monthly "Building Services Fee" of $175, which may be increased annually at Landlord's discretion.

3.3 Tenant shall pay a $50 monthly "Administrative Fee" for processing rent payments.

3.4 Tenant shall pay $200 per move-in/move-out for elevator usage and cleaning, regardless of actual use.

## 4. PETS

4.1 No pets are permitted without Landlord's prior written consent.

4.2 If pets are permitted, Tenant shall pay:
   - Non-refundable pet fee of $750 per pet
   - Monthly pet rent of $125 per pet
   - Additional security deposit of $1,500 per pet

4.3 Pet privileges may be revoked at any time at Landlord's sole discretion.

## 5. RULES AND REGULATIONS

5.1 Tenant agrees to comply with all Building Rules and Regulations, which are incorporated herein by reference.

5.2 Landlord may amend, modify, or supplement the Rules and Regulations at any time, in its sole and absolute discretion, and such amendments shall be binding upon Tenant upon notice.

5.3 Violation of any Rule may result in a fine of up to $500 per violation per day, in Landlord's sole discretion.

## 6. ENTRY

6.1 Landlord shall have the right to enter the Premises at any reasonable time, with or without notice, for purposes of inspection, repairs, or showing the unit.

6.2 In case of emergency, Landlord may enter without notice at any time.

## 7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

7.1 Tenant is responsible for all repairs and maintenance to the Premises, including but not limited to plumbing, electrical, appliances, HVAC, and structural components.

7.2 Tenant must report any damage immediately. Failure to report shall make Tenant responsible for all resulting damage, regardless of cause.

7.3 Repairs costing less than $500 shall be the sole responsibility of Tenant.

7.4 Tenant shall be responsible for professional pest control, performed at minimum every six months at Tenant's expense.

## 8. EARLY TERMINATION

8.1 If Tenant terminates this Lease before the end of the Term, Tenant shall pay:
   - All remaining rent due under the Lease (in a lump sum, due immediately upon termination)
   - A termination fee equal to two (2) months' rent
   - All costs of re-leasing including advertising, broker fees, and lost rent
   - Forfeiture of the entire security deposit

8.2 Landlord shall have no duty to mitigate damages or attempt to re-rent the Premises.

## 9. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT

9.1 Tenant may not sublet, assign, or share the Premises with any other person without Landlord's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Landlord's sole discretion.

9.2 Any unauthorized occupancy shall result in immediate termination and forfeiture of the security deposit.

## 10. LIABILITY AND WAIVER

10.1 Tenant waives any and all claims against Landlord for personal injury, property damage, or any other losses occurring in or about the Premises or building, except in cases of Landlord's gross negligence.

10.2 Landlord shall not be liable for any failure or interruption of utilities or building services.

10.3 Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless Landlord from any claims arising from Tenant's use of the Premises.

## 11. RENEWAL

11.1 If Tenant remains in possession after the Lease Term ends without a new written lease, the tenancy shall automatically convert to a month-to-month tenancy at 125% of the prior rent.

11.2 During such holdover period, Landlord may terminate the tenancy with thirty (30) days notice, but Tenant must give sixty (60) days notice.

## 12. ATTORNEY FEES

12.1 In any action arising from this Lease, the prevailing party shall be entitled to attorney fees and costs.

12.2 Notwithstanding the foregoing, if Landlord prevails, Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable attorney fees. If Tenant prevails, each party shall bear its own costs.

## 13. ARBITRATION

13.1 Any disputes arising from this Lease shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration in New York, NY, administered by a private arbitration service of Landlord's choosing.

13.2 Tenant waives any right to a jury trial or to participate in class actions.

## 14. ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

14.1 Tenant acknowledges receipt of all required disclosures and the right to inspect the Premises.

14.2 Tenant has had the opportunity to review this Lease with an attorney.

14.3 Tenant waives any right to cancel this Lease after signing.
