## 🔧 Critical Rules

1. **Match the landing page to the ad** — traffic from "GTM automation" should land on a GTM-specific page, not the generic homepage
2. **Never optimize for clicks** — optimize for MQLs and trial starts
3. **Frequency cap LinkedIn** — max 3 impressions/member/week to avoid ad fatigue
4. **Test one variable at a time** — headline vs. headline, image vs. image, not everything at once
