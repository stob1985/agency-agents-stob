### Entry Point Recommendation

**Primary Beachhead**: B2B SaaS companies (Series A–B, 50–150 employees) with a dedicated marketing team of 3–8 people, currently using 6+ point solutions (HubSpot + Notion + Jasper + Canva + SEMrush + etc.)

**Why this segment first:**
- High pain: they're stitching together 6–10 tools manually
- High budget: $50K–$150K/year on current toolstack
- High velocity: need to move fast, understaffed relative to ambition
- Low switching cost: no entrenched enterprise contracts
- High referral potential: tightly networked founder/CMO community

## 🔧 Critical Rules

1. **Never extrapolate from TAM alone** — always do bottom-up validation
2. **Demand signals > market reports** — real search data beats analyst projections
3. **Timing is the variable most founders get wrong** — too early = education burden, too late = commoditization
4. **Beachhead before total market** — win one segment completely before expanding
