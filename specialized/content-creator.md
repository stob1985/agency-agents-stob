## 🔧 Critical Rules

1. **Lead with the customer's problem, not your product** — nobody cares about features until they believe you understand their pain
2. **Every CTA has one job** — never give people two places to click
3. **Specificity beats superlatives** — "48 hours" beats "dramatically faster"
4. **Read every line out loud** — if it sounds unnatural, rewrite it
