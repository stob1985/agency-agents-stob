# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Effective Date:** March 1, 2026
**Engagement Value:** $50,000 (estimated)

This Independent Contractor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

**CLIENT:** Apex Holdings Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation, 1 Park Avenue, New York, NY 10016
**CONTRACTOR:** [CONTRACTOR NAME]

---

## 1. SERVICES

1.1 Contractor shall provide software development, design, consulting, and any related services as requested by Client from time to time ("Services").

1.2 The scope of Services may be expanded at Client's sole discretion at no additional cost to Client.

1.3 Contractor shall be available for work no less than 40 hours per week and shall be reachable by Client during normal business hours (9:00 AM - 7:00 PM Eastern Time, Monday through Saturday).

## 2. COMPENSATION

2.1 Client shall pay Contractor at the rate of $75 per hour, billed monthly in arrears.

2.2 Invoices are payable Net Ninety (90) days from receipt. Client may dispute any portion of any invoice at any time, including after payment.

2.3 Client may withhold up to 25% of any invoice as a "performance reserve," to be released upon final acceptance of all deliverables.

2.4 Contractor is responsible for all taxes, including any taxes Client may be required to withhold or remit on Contractor's behalf, and shall indemnify Client for any such taxes.

## 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

3.1 All work product, deliverables, code, designs, ideas, inventions, discoveries, concepts, know-how, and any other intellectual property created, conceived, or developed by Contractor during the term of this Agreement, **whether or not related to the Services**, shall be the sole and exclusive property of Client.

3.2 Contractor hereby assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to any pre-existing intellectual property of Contractor that is incorporated into or used in connection with the Services.

3.3 Contractor agrees to execute any documents and take any actions necessary to perfect Client's ownership rights, at Contractor's expense.

3.4 Contractor waives all moral rights in any work product.

## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 Contractor shall hold all Client information in strict confidence in perpetuity.

4.2 The definition of "Confidential Information" includes anything Contractor learns, observes, or has access to in connection with this Agreement, whether marked confidential or not.

4.3 Contractor's confidentiality obligations survive termination indefinitely.

## 5. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION

5.1 During the term of this Agreement and for a period of **twenty-four (24) months** thereafter, Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the world:
   (a) provide services similar to the Services to any business that competes with Client;
   (b) work for, consult for, or invest in any competitor of Client;
   (c) solicit or accept work from any Client customer, prospect, vendor, or employee.

5.2 The restrictions in this Section 5 are reasonable and necessary, and Contractor waives any defense based on overbreadth or unenforceability.

## 6. INDEMNIFICATION

6.1 Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client and its officers, directors, employees, agents, and affiliates from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including attorney fees) arising from or related to:
   (a) Contractor's performance or non-performance of the Services;
   (b) any breach of this Agreement by Contractor;
   (c) any third-party claim of any nature, including IP infringement, related to the Services;
   (d) any act or omission of Contractor.

6.2 Contractor's indemnification obligations are uncapped and survive termination.

## 7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL CLIENT'S LIABILITY TO CONTRACTOR UNDER THIS AGREEMENT EXCEED ONE HUNDRED DOLLARS ($100), REGARDLESS OF THE FORM OF ACTION, INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE.

7.2 CLIENT SHALL NOT BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS.

7.3 The limitations in this Section 7 apply notwithstanding any failure of essential purpose of any limited remedy.

## 8. TERMINATION

8.1 Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, with no notice and no further obligation.

8.2 Contractor may terminate this Agreement only upon ninety (90) days prior written notice, and only if Contractor has completed all work in progress to Client's satisfaction.

8.3 Upon termination by Contractor, Contractor forfeits all unpaid amounts and shall pay Client a transition fee equal to two months' average billing.

## 9. RELATIONSHIP

9.1 Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of Client. Contractor shall not be entitled to any benefits afforded to Client's employees.

9.2 Notwithstanding the foregoing, Contractor shall comply with all Client policies, procedures, dress codes, and workplace rules.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles.

10.2 Any dispute shall be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware. Contractor consents to personal jurisdiction therein and waives any objection based on forum non conveniens.

10.3 The prevailing party in any dispute shall be entitled to attorney fees, except that if Contractor prevails, each party shall bear its own fees.

10.4 Contractor waives any right to a jury trial and any right to participate in any class or collective action.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

11.1 Client may modify the terms of this Agreement at any time upon written notice to Contractor. Contractor's continued performance after such notice shall constitute acceptance.

11.2 This Agreement may be assigned by Client without Contractor's consent. Contractor may not assign this Agreement.

11.3 If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.

11.4 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, whether written or oral.
