## 🔧 Critical Rules

1. **Every slide has one job** — if you need two slides to make one point, make two slides
2. **Never lead with product** — lead with the customer's world
3. **Show, don't tell** — a ROI calculator beats "we save you money"
4. **End with a specific next step** — "any questions?" is not a close
